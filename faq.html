Process

It starts with understanding the project — the sound, the vision, the audience, and what needs to be delivered. From there, a direction is put together and work begins. Drafts or previews are shared at key stages so nothing goes too far in the wrong direction before it gets corrected.

Delivery

It depends on the project size. A simple logo or graphic can be done in a few days. A visualiser usually takes 3–7 days. A full music video or brand package can take 1–3 weeks depending on complexity and revision rounds. Timelines are confirmed when the quote is sent.

That depends on what the project needs. Videos are typically delivered as high-resolution MP4 files. Design work comes as PNG, JPEG, or PDF. Source files like PSD or AI can be included depending on what was agreed. Just specify what formats are needed when booking.

Files are sent via Google Drive, WeTransfer, or whatever platform works best. Large video files especially need a proper transfer method — not just email. The delivery method gets sorted out during the project.

Revisions

The number of revision rounds is confirmed in the quote before work begins. Starter projects typically include light revision rounds, while Pro and Premium packages include more. Major changes that go beyond the original brief may affect the final price.

A revision is a refinement of what was agreed — adjusting a colour, tweaking text, trimming a clip. A new request is changing the concept, adding scenes, or switching the direction entirely. New requests are treated as additional work and quoted separately.

Feedback is always welcome — that's what the revision rounds are for. The clearer the feedback is, the faster the fix. If the direction needs a bigger change, that conversation happens openly and a solution gets worked out. The goal is always work that the artist is genuinely happy with.

Yes. For projects that need to stay private before release — unreleased music, upcoming album campaigns, or confidential brand work — an NDA can be arranged. Reach out when booking and this gets sorted before any work begins.
