Good to Know

A Few Tips Before We Arrive

⚠️
Separate Whites & Colours
Please separate your white clothes from your colourful ones before pickup. This prevents colour bleeding and ensures the best results for every garment.
🏷️
Check Care Labels
If any item has a special care label (dry clean only, delicate, etc.), please flag it when booking or leave a note — we'll treat it accordingly.
👟
Shoes Cleaned Separately
Shoes are handled as a separate service from clothing. Add it to your basket order or book it on its own — either way we collect everything together.
📅
Book in Advance for Express
For 24-hour turnaround, book your pickup before 10am. Orders placed after midday are treated as the following day's collection.
💬
WhatsApp Us Anytime
Prefer to chat? You can place orders, ask questions, or track your laundry directly via WhatsApp — we're always available.
💳
Cash or EFT on Delivery
We collect payment when your clean laundry arrives. No upfront payment, no surprises — just easy, trusted service.
